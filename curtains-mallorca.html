Curtains made for your windows

Everything we make is measured, cut and sewn for one specific window. Nothing comes off a shelf and gets trimmed to fit. We design it with you, make it in our own workshop and fit it ourselves, so there is no third party in the middle and nobody else to chase.

Mallorca light is not gentle. It is bright, low in winter and hard on fabrics in summer, and a curtain that works beautifully in a flat in London can look wrong here. Choosing well is mostly about seeing the fabric in the room it will live in, at the hour of day you will actually be there.

How the mobile showroom works

It is a shop turned inside out. Instead of you coming to see fabrics under artificial light, we bring the entire sample collection to your home — and you decide with the fabric in your hand, against your walls, in your light.

01

You get in touch

By WhatsApp, phone or the form below. Tell us roughly what you have in mind and where you are on the island, and we agree a day and a time that suits you.

02

We come to you

About 45 minutes. We arrive with the full sample collection and lay it out in the room itself, so you see and touch each fabric in your own natural light.

03

We measure properly

Laser measuring, window by window. Old Mallorcan houses are rarely square, and two centimetres is the difference between a curtain that hangs and one that does not.

04

Your quote in 24 hours

Itemised, in writing, with no obligation. If you go ahead we make everything in our own workshop and come back to fit it. Installation is included.

And you have not left the house.

Questions we get most often

I only come to the island a few times a year. Can you still help?

Yes, and a good part of our work is exactly that. We can fit the visit around the dates you are here, or arrange access with whoever holds the keys — a neighbour, a property manager, your builder — and keep you updated by WhatsApp and email while you are away. Decisions on fabric can be made on a video call once we have shown you the samples in the room.

Which parts of Mallorca do you cover?

The whole island. There is no coverage radius: the showroom travels to wherever you are, from central Palma to any village. Menorca, Ibiza and Formentera on request.

How long does the visit take, and does it cost anything?

Around 45 minutes, and it is free. The visit, the advice and the measuring carry no cost and no obligation, anywhere on the island. You get a detailed written quote within 24 hours.

Do you install, or do I need to find a fitter?

We install. Everything is made in our own workshop and fitted by us, so the measuring, the making and the hanging are all the same responsibility. Nothing is subcontracted and there is nobody to chase.

Can you work with my architect, interior designer or builder?

Yes, we do it regularly, and it is usually the easiest way when a renovation is already under way. We can coordinate directly with them on measurements, timings and access.

What about a rental property or a hotel?

We do a lot of it. For holiday lets and hotels the priorities are different — blackout that genuinely blocks light, fabrics that survive sun and frequent washing, and fitting scheduled outside the season. Tell us the number of rooms and the dates you need it done by.

Why do you not have a shop?

Because a shop asks you to choose under fluorescent light, with the fabric taken out of context. Choosing a curtain is really choosing how light comes into your home, and that can only be judged properly at your own window.
